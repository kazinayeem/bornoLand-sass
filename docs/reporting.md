# BornoLand Centralized Reporting Architecture

## 1. Unified Mathematical Formulations

To eliminate duplicate or conflicting financial and operational numbers across POS, Dashboard, and Reports, all calculations follow single canonical formulas:

### A. True Cost & Gross Margin
$$\text{True Cost} = \text{Buying Price} + \text{Landed Cost} + \text{Packaging} + \text{Waste Provision} + \text{Other Costs}$$
$$\text{Gross Profit} = \text{Net Revenue} - \text{COGS}$$
$$\text{Gross Margin \%} = \left(\frac{\text{Gross Profit}}{\text{Net Revenue}}\right) \times 100$$

### B. Double-Entry Accounting
$$\sum \text{Debits} = \sum \text{Credits}$$
$$\text{Assets} = \text{Liabilities} + \text{Equity}$$
$$\text{Net Operating Income} = \text{Total Operating Revenue} - \text{COGS} - \text{Operating Expenses}$$

### C. Cash Drawer Reconciliation (POS)
$$\text{Cash Variance} = \text{Actual Counted Cash} - \text{Expected Drawer Cash}$$
$$\text{Expected Drawer Cash} = \text{Opening Float} + \text{Cash Sales} - \text{Cash Refunds}$$

### D. Payroll Net Disbursement
$$\text{Net Salary} = (\text{Basic Salary} + \text{Allowances} + \text{Overtime Pay}) - (\text{Tax Withheld} + \text{Provident Fund} + \text{Unpaid Leaves})$$

## 2. Standardized Reports Catalog

1. **Commerce & Sales Reports**: Daily/Weekly/Monthly Sales Breakdown, Sales by Channel (Online vs POS), Product Performance, Top Grossing Categories.
2. **Operations & Inventory Reports**: Stock Movement Audit Ledger, Stock Valuation by Warehouse, Waste & Damage Write-Off Summary, Supplier Purchase History.
3. **Workforce & HR Reports**: Monthly Attendance Summary, Overtime Log, Approved vs Rejected Leaves, Total Payroll Disbursement.
4. **Financial Statements**:
   - **Trial Balance**: Debit and credit equality verification across all account codes.
   - **Profit & Loss (P&L)**: Operating revenue, cost of goods sold, operating expenses, and net profit.
   - **Balance Sheet**: Assets (Current & Non-Current), Liabilities (Accounts Payable & Payroll Due), and Equity.
